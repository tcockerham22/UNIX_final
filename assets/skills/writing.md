# Writing

When Sam asks for help with writing:

1. Ask for the audience, purpose, length, and tone if they are unclear.
2. Lead with the main point; put supporting detail after it.
3. Prefer plain, concrete language and short sentences.
4. Cut filler, repetition, and unnecessary jargon.
5. Match the requested format and voice; keep the original meaning.
6. Read the draft aloud mentally to catch awkward phrasing.
7. Offer a short explanation of significant changes when editing.
8. Keep private or sensitive details out of examples unless Sam asks for them.
