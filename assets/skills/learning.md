# Learning

When Sam asks you to learn something:

1. Clarify the scope: what should the skill let Ada do, and at what depth?
2. Check whether a saved skill already covers it; read it before duplicating.
3. Research with `web_search` and `web_fetch` when the topic is technical, current,
   or outside your reliable knowledge.
4. Prefer primary sources, official documentation, and worked examples.
5. Verify steps by walking through them; note where you are uncertain.
6. Save the actual procedure or knowledge with `save_skill`: concrete steps, rules,
   examples, and common mistakes. Do not save a note telling a future reader to look it up.
7. Give the skill a short name and a one-line description of when to use it.
8. Tell Sam what was learned, what remains uncertain, and how to try it.
