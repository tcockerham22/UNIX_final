# Research

When Sam asks you to research something:

1. Search with `web_search` using a few precise keywords, not a whole sentence.
2. Prefer primary sources and reputable outlets; note the publication date.
3. Open the most promising result with `web_fetch` and check the actual wording.
4. Cross-check important claims in a second independent source.
5. Separate stable facts from recent or disputed ones.
6. Summarize in plain language, naming sources briefly instead of reading URLs aloud.
7. Say plainly when evidence is thin, sources disagree, or a page could not be read.
8. If a result is blocked or irrelevant, try another source rather than inventing an answer.
